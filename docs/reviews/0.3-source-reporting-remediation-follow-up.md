## Finding closure

- **F1 / P2 — closed:** `ScalarQuantity` is now exported through the package
  root. The reviewer independently confirmed exact parity among the 93-name
  `__all__`, the hardcoded test inventory, and the marked consumer-document
  inventory. A standalone strict-mypy consumer probe and a clean installed
  wheel could import and use the alias without a private engine import or a
  direct Pint dependency.
- **F2 / P2 — closed:** the changelog and consumer guide now distinguish both
  published 0.2.0 pH compatibility paths. `ReportedPH` convenience constructors
  remain numeric while stored/direct-field values and `calculation_value` use
  FermUnits `PHValue`; `TargetWaterProfile.ph` construction requires
  `PHValue`. The reviewer confirmed those claims against the `v0.2.0` tag and
  FermUnits 0.1.3.

The revised reported-pH example uses 7.4 as the reported average for a
7.2-through-7.8 range, so it cannot pass by confusing the reported value with
the forbidden arithmetic midpoint of 7.5. Range-only pH remains without a
calculation value, finite pH outside 0 through 14 remains representable, and
calculated working-water pH remains explicitly unsupported.

## Treatment-material documentation result

The reviewer found the later documentation commit scientifically accurate,
internally consistent, and unambiguous about current versus future behavior. It
correctly distinguishes:

- ideal chemical identity from a real treatment material;
- hydration state from commercial purity or assay;
- solid assay from explicitly based solution concentration;
- mass dosing from density-dependent volume dosing;
- equilibrium solubility from dissolution kinetics; and
- complete-dissolution stoichiometry from carbonate/CO2 equilibrium behavior.

The review independently checked the calcium-chloride, gypsum, carbonate, and
alkalinity sources. It found that the roadmap appropriately places practical
treatment-material semantics before optimization and that the chalk and
low-mineral dilution boundaries do not overstate implemented capability.

The reviewer considered removal of the earlier ProMash, BrewSession, and Bru'n
Water discovery entries non-defective because they supported no implemented
scientific claim and the generalized research task remains. A suggested
explanation for the Bru'n Water removal was optional and is not needed as a
lasting scientific-policy statement.
