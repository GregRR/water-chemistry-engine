# Water Chemistry Engine 0.5 Total Alkalinity — Pass 1 Review

This record summarizes the independent review of commit `d2fcda4`, the first
implemented `conservative_equivalent_alkalinity_v1` slice. The reviewer
inspected the change and independently recomputed the sodium-bicarbonate and
reporting-basis examples. The reviewer did not execute the repository test
suite.

This is a point-in-time audit artifact, not a normative specification. Current
behavior is defined by the code, tests, and active design documentation.

## Review conclusion

The reviewer judged the slice **ready after minor corrections**. Source
resolution, unknown propagation, zero-volume handling, NaHCO3 equivalent
accounting, target comparison, separation from carbonate-species inventory,
public exports, and continued optimizer/pH exclusions were confirmed correct.

## Findings and adjudication

### F1 — High — equivalent-mass precision change lacked migration notice

**Confirmed and remediated.** The new model centralized the CaCO3 equivalent
mass at 50.04345 g/eq, refining the earlier 50.04 g/eq value used by
`bicarbonate_from_bicarbonate_alkalinity_as_caco3`. That changed every result
from the existing helper slightly, but the changelog did not identify the
behavior change and the helper still described only the rounded USGS value.

The remediation:

- adds a 0.5 compatibility warning with the old and new 100 mg/L worked result;
- documents the 100.0869 g/mol divided-by-two derivation and its consistency
  with the approximately 50.04 g/eq USGS value;
- updates the equation in the helper docstring; and
- pins both 50.04345 and its formula-mass derivation in a regression test.

### F2 — Medium — alkalinity contribution-matrix branches lacked coverage

**Confirmed and remediated.** The original test covered only a known,
positive-volume source. A regression test now combines a zero-volume unknown
source, positive-volume unknown source, and positive-volume resolved source and
checks all three source-cell statuses, unresolved blend/final totals, the known
weighted contribution, and the reviewed treatment contribution.

### F3 — Low — modeled alkalinity permits negative values

**Not a defect in the current contract.** All currently implemented inputs and
treatment contributions are nonnegative, so a negative modeled result is
unreachable today. The active design deliberately anticipates future signed
acid/base contributions and states that calculated alkalinity must not be
silently clamped to zero. The appropriate validity policy will be decided with
that future reaction model rather than imposing a premature nonnegative output
invariant now.

### F4 — Low — above-target and boundary-tolerance cases lacked direct tests

**Confirmed as inexpensive hardening and remediated.** New cases verify a
positive signed deviation above an alkalinity range and within-target handling
for floating-point noise inside the documented 1e-9 mg/L boundary tolerance.

## Confirmed exclusions

- Bicarbonate and carbonate remain formal ion inventory, not alkalinity-derived
  equilibrium species.
- Sodium bicarbonate remains unavailable for automatic optimizer selection.
- Optimizer alkalinity admission and post-rounding replay remain pending.
- Calculated working-water pH remains unsupported.
