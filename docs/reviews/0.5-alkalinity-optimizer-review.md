## Finding and adjudication

### F1 — Medium — mixed-objective policy disclosure was incomplete

**Confirmed and remediated.** The optimizer consistently includes absolute
total-alkalinity deviation in mg/L as CaCO3 in the same unweighted ranking sum
as ion deviations in mg/L. The implementation is internally consistent, but
the existing policy disclaimer referred only to comparison across ions and did
not explicitly disclose this additional reporting-basis distinction.

The roadmap and consumer guide now state that the mixed ion/alkalinity sum is a
versioned project ranking convenience. They explicitly reject any inference
that a unit of ion-mass deviation and a unit of equivalent-mass alkalinity
deviation are scientifically interchangeable. The changelog records the
clarification.

No implementation change was required, and no new defect was found.
