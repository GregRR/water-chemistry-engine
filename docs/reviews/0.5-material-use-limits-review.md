### L1 — Low — below-limit acceptance lacked a direct regression test

**Confirmed as a coverage gap and remediated.** A request whose explicit

### L2 — Low — alternate blend-policy enforcement lacked direct tests

**Confirmed as a coverage gap and remediated.** Above-limit rejection is now
tested across fixed blend, proportional dilution, and bounded source-volume
selection. The shared validation remains independent of blend policy.

### L3 — Low — returned-plan policy provenance lacked direct proof

**Confirmed as a coverage gap and remediated.** An end-to-end optimizer test
now proves that the exact selected policy is reachable through the returned
plan's material addition and constraint.

### L4 — Low — calculation-volume failure and conversion paths lacked tests

**Confirmed as a coverage gap and remediated.** Tests now cover nonfinite and
wrong-dimension calculation volumes plus equivalent liter/milliliter batch
scaling. Boundary-tolerance behavior immediately around the policy maximum is
also pinned directly.

### L5 — Low observation — policy volume/process basis is free text

**Accepted as a future admission gate, not a current defect.** No built-in
numeric policy exists, the caller must select an applicable policy, and the
required applicability statement records that judgment. Before the first
reusable numeric policy is admitted, the project will add a structured,
machine-checkable volume-basis/water-stage identity compatible with optimizer
total-volume semantics. The roadmap and research priorities now record that
gate.

No production-code defect was found.
